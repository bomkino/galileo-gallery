# S0 charter candidate — The Build

Status: **G10C preflight candidate only**; human verdict pending; production implementation blocked.

## Memorable outcome

A finished slide is presented through a deliberate sequence of Project-authored construction stages—only when those stages genuinely exist—so the audience can understand an authored progression rather than watch decorative pieces fly together.

## Current honest mode

This packet implements only `flat-source-preflight`. The imported source remains one indivisible plane. Project-known presentation apparatus may enter in a diagnostic phrase around it: world backing, frame/matte, guides, caption slot, timing cursor, and source frame. These are not represented as the creator’s historical process.

`authored-stages` remains blocked behind a future versioned Project contract that supplies explicit stage identity, order, duration, source provenance, transforms, and round-trip semantics.

## Emotional register

Confident, authored, legible, deliberate. More title-sequence construction than software tutorial.

## Motion sentence

Known stages arrive with purpose, settle into one readable composition, hold long enough to understand the result, then clear or return without teleporting, fake approval, or invented authorship.

## Anti-goals

- No fabricated palette exploration.
- No fake typography trials.
- No generated wireframe passed off as prior process.
- No “READY,” “APPROVED,” checkmark, stamp, cursor, or software chrome unless explicitly authored as a stage.
- No semantic segmentation of a flattened source.
- No random flying fragments or generic assembly engine.
- No source tint, shadow, glow, grain, blur, or relighting.
- No claim of G10C completion from this preflight.

## Composition

The final source occupies a stable hero plane. Preflight apparatus lives around or behind it and uses consistent stage-safe coordinates. Each present stage owns a stable identity. Arrival paths do not cross. Finale establishes the intact source at full readability. Portrait, square, and wide canvases recompose the staging lanes without rotating the source world.

## Time

Default diagnostic phrase: **11.6 seconds**. Readable floor: **7.9 seconds**. Shorter fixed durations fail rather than crushing stages into unreadable flashes.

Automatic mode uses one preflight phrase. Fixed-duration mode retimes it exactly above the dynamic floor. Directed mode compiles fast ×2, fast ×2, regular ×1, fast ×1 complete phrases. Reverse uses the exact inverse stage paths and does not change authored order metadata.

Reduced motion presents the final source with a static stage ledger; it does not rapidly reveal all apparatus.

## Controls

Only controls grounded in actual capability: stage pace, finale hold, presentation scale, direction, fit, labels, and background. Authored-stage selection appears only when the future contract exists. No randomisation, stage count invention, source effect, palette, cursor, stamp, or fake-process controls.

## Accessibility and lifecycle

Expose current stage name, stage count, total duration, final hold, and preflight limitation in text. Keyboard play/pause, previous/next stage, Home/End, and direct finale. Minimum 44 px controls. Dispose animation, observers, media seeks, listeners, and generated URLs cleanly.

## Acceptance boundary

This packet may validate timing language, stage identity, source restraint, UI journey, and contract questions. It does not integrate The Build, satisfy G10C, freeze G10D, package a Product Scene, or claim human acceptance.

# Source fidelity, alpha, and Look — The Build

## Binding source rule

The source is one intact imported media plane. Gallery may reveal or hide that plane through a presentation window, but must not reconstruct, recolour, relight, relabel, segment, or semantically interpret its pixels.

At every visible sample:

- artwork opacity: `1`;
- filter: `none`;
- blend mode: `normal`;
- source canvas/image bytes: unchanged;
- fit/alignment: current honest Product capability;
- focal point: unavailable because the current Project schema has no settled per-source focal field.

## Resolved-state pixel contract

During `resolved-hold`:

- source reveal equals `1`;
- guides and cursor equal `0` opacity;
- caption appears only when it is actual Project caption data;
- no overlay crosses source pixels except the declared frame edge outside the content rect;
- source hash equals its initial generated-fixture hash;
- source equality is expected where resampling permits.

## Flat-source honesty

Permitted presentation apparatus:

- neutral frame/backing;
- crop/content window;
- canvas/frame safe-area guides;
- placement marks;
- actual Project caption;
- optional causal cursor during source placement only.

Forbidden inferred content:

- image/type/texture/brand/polish layers;
- palette extraction presented as authored process;
- type trials;
- approval, ready, sign-off, or authorship labels;
- hidden design rationale;
- arbitrary stages derived from extra media.

## Transparent canvas

Candidate support: yes.

- Empty pixels use straight alpha.
- Pixels with alpha `0` must have RGB `(0,0,0)`.
- Guide/cursor pixels may be non-zero only where visibly painted.
- Source transparency remains source transparency.
- No paper, vignette, grain, glow, tint, or light wash is forced behind the source.

Evidence must include straight-alpha capture and composites over black, white, red, blue, and checkerboard.

## Look boundary

The atelier does not prebuild G10D. The Scene may expose neutral apparatus geometry; colour/material choices remain Look-owned later. No bloom, rays, particles, warp, ripple, caustics, confetti, texture, or lighting flourish is identity-critical.

## Video and failed media

- Video source samples Product story time. Prototype-generated video fixture demonstrates state identity only.
- Failed source retains ID, order, role, and unavailable presentation. It never exposes an extra item under the primary identity.
- Audio remains Product-owned and unchanged.

# Source fidelity, alpha, and Look — The Build

## No fabricated process

The candidate may not derive palette swatches, typography variants, layout trials, masks, wireframes, approval marks, cursor paths, or semantic layers from a flattened source. Generated process theatre is worse than missing capability because it misrepresents authorship.

The intact source plane always renders with opacity 1, filter `none`, normal blend, and no Scene-authored tint, exposure change, blur, sharpen, grain, vignette, border, shadow, glow, sweep, texture, or material treatment. Contain is the Clean default.

## Preflight apparatus

World, matte, guides, caption slot, stage ledger, and labels are separate Project/UI layers. They may arrive around or behind the source to test staging. They must not be described as historical design steps. At finale, review-only apparatus clears or remains outside source bounds so the final slide is fully readable.

## Authored stages

A future authored-stage input may contain actual source-backed stage media or explicit construction data. Each stage requires its own provenance and source-fidelity policy. The Scene may not flatten or alter those stages without user intent. Stage absence is not filled by a generated substitute.

## Alpha

Transparent source pixels remain transparent. In a transparent world, alpha-zero output pixels must retain zero RGB and no guide, label, grain, or backing colour may contaminate them. The preflight prototype can test generated straight-alpha fixtures but does not claim Product export alpha integration.

## Failed source or stage

A failed source retains its stage identity and final position as a causal placeholder. A failed authored stage in a future contract must retain its order and dependency consequence. Failure never causes later stages to be renumbered silently.

## Look boundary

The future shared Look may style the world behind stages and non-source review apparatus. It may not relight the assembled object if that changes artwork. Stable luminance, deterministic subtle phase, decorrelated world grain, and transparent-output sanitation belong to G10D. The Build must not prebuild them.

Audio remains Project-owned and unchanged.

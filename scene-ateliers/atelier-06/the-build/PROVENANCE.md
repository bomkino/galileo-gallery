# Provenance — The Build

## Classification

Clean-room G10C preflight candidate. Not a production Scene and not an authored-process claim.

## Inputs inspected

- live Gallery product decisions and implementation status;
- live catalogue promise for an authored build;
- common Scene Atelier contract;
- current Project, Timeline, source-fidelity, Look, audio, export, accessibility, and lifecycle boundaries;
- observable construction-sequence principles, constrained to explicit Project-known stages.

## Historical source use

No historical `TheBuild` implementation file was opened or adapted. No `R03-the-build` packet was instantiated. No historical code, comments, constants, identifiers, assets, CSS, component structure, timing values, palette, cursor, stamp, or wireframe were copied.

## Process restraint

No OCR, segmentation, computer vision, generative reconstruction, design-file inference, or source-pixel analysis was used to invent stages. The candidate keeps a flattened source intact and labels all surrounding stage work as preflight apparatus.

## External resources

None. The prototype loads no network code, fonts, media, analytics, or telemetry. Fixtures are locally generated and declarative.

## Future authored-stage provenance

Every future stage must identify its source, author, ownership/licence status, hash or stable content identity, and relationship to the final composition. Presence inside a Project is not itself proof of publication rights.

## Rights boundary

This packet does not grant rights over user media, source design files, historical assets, or future authored stages. Product integration must maintain a separate provenance and licence ledger.

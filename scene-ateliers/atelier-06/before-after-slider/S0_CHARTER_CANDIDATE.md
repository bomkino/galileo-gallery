# S0 charter candidate — Before / After

Status: candidate; human verdict pending.

## Memorable outcome

Two explicitly related source frames occupy one stable comparison plane. A precise divider reveals their difference without dissolving identity, hiding evidence, or turning the Scene into an editor.

## Emotional register

Clear, forensic, satisfying, restrained.

## Motion sentence

The divider crosses the shared frame, settles at readable holds, reverses along the same path, and closes an invisible ping-pong seam.

## Anti-goals

- No fake pair made by duplicating one source.
- No crossfade masquerading as comparison.
- No hard reset from 100% to 0%.
- No source tint, sharpening, exposure change, or colour grading.
- No automatic registration, warping, cropping, or “AI enhancement.”
- No silent discard of third and later Project items.
- No draggable handle in export unless a separate interactive-output product exists.

## Source contract

Exactly two semantic roles are required: `before` and `after`. They remain stable for the full phrase. One item is an honest insufficient-input state. Extra items remain preserved by the Project but are outside this Scene evaluation and must produce an explicit consequence rather than silent loss.

Both sources share the same visible comparison box. Clean default is contain. If ratios differ, letterboxing is honest. Cover/crop requires explicit user intent per side or a future shared registration contract.

## Time

Automatic mode performs a continuous ping-pong reveal with readable endpoint holds. Fixed-duration mode retimes the same complete phrase exactly. Directed mode compiles fast ×2, fast ×2, regular ×1, fast ×1 comparison phrases. Reverse swaps travel direction, not source role. The seam is physically identical.

Reduced motion presents a stable 50/50 split with labelled sides. Manual keyboard adjustment remains immediate and non-animated.

## Controls

- reveal direction;
- divider orientation;
- endpoint hold;
- divider treatment;
- source fit;
- labels on/off;
- comparison starting side.

No saturation, exposure, contrast, sharpening, blur, crop automation, source opacity, or glow control.

## Output boundary

Candidate output is opaque. Alpha comparison is deferred until straight-alpha overlap, premultiplication, edge-fringe, and transparent-background semantics are separately chartered and tested.

## Accessibility

The comparison state is exposed numerically as a percentage and textually as visible before/after regions. Keyboard: Space play/pause; arrows adjust reveal; Home/End move to endpoints; `0` returns to 50/50. Minimum 44 px controls. Live announcements occur only on explicit actions and endpoint arrival.

## Acceptance boundary

Prototype evidence may prove two-role validation, continuous reversal, exact duration, keyboard semantics, source-clean rendering, and failure preservation. It does not prove registration accuracy, final Look, Product integration, packaging, or human comprehension.

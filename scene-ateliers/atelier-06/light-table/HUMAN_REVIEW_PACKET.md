## Review at real speed

Open `prototype/index.html`, use the ordinary-six fixture, and watch one complete automatic phrase at 1× before scrubbing. Then compare one, five, six, and twenty-four sources at 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, and 4:5.

## Questions requiring human judgment

1. **Identity:** Does the first second read as a working illuminated table, not a Contact Sheet with rotations?
2. **Stillness:** Is motion subordinate to inspection throughout?
3. **Count:** Is six the right ordinary default, with one remaining valid and five intentionally airier?
4. **Overlap:** Do frames feel handled without compromising source legibility?
5. **Under-light:** Does light visibly originate behind/around frames without seeming to brighten the artwork?
6. **Loupe:** Does inspection remain part of a working set rather than becoming Vitrine?
7. **Pace:** Are holds long enough to inspect at 1×? Are fast directed transits still readable?
8. **Reduced motion:** Does the static focus treatment retain the Scene's meaning?
9. **Neighbour gate:** In silhouette and real speed, is this clearly different from Contact Sheet and Lively Prints?
10. **Opaque policy:** Is refusing alpha the correct honest boundary for this identity?

## Blockers to approval

Reject or revise if:

- light appears inside or over source pixels;
- five and six look like accidental variants rather than deliberate policies;
- one source looks like a fake pile;
- focus route reads as a carousel;
- drift becomes the main attraction;
- any remount changes layout;
- source colour changes at focus;
- the opaque-only consequence is hidden.

## Provisional atelier recommendation

Advance to formal S0 review only with minimum `1`, ordinary `6`, opaque-only output, serialized interaction exclusion, and the source-isolation tests intact. Human verdict remains pending.

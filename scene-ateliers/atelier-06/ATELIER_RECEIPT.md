## Decisions

### Light Table

A restrained illuminated review surface. It never becomes a draggable scrapbook or a generic contact sheet. Minimum one item, default six, bounded visible many twenty-four. The loupe advances through stable media identities; missing media retain their cell and order. Light exists behind or around artwork only.

### Before / After

A reversible two-source comparison instrument. It never duplicates one source into a false comparison and never silently discards extras. The divider follows one continuous ping-pong path. Default output remains opaque until a separate alpha-comparison contract exists.

### Slide Anatomy

A truthful explanation of one slide’s presentation structure. Clean-room default is `flat-source`: Project-known matte, guides, caption apparatus, and source frame may separate; semantic image layers are never inferred. The assembly returns along the same path.

### The Build

A G10C preflight packet, not a production Scene. It may stage Project-known apparatus around a flat source but must not fabricate process history, palette trials, typography alternatives, approval stamps, or authorship. Default diagnostic phrase is 11.6 seconds; the readable floor is 7.9 seconds.

World treatment remains behind or around media. Transparent pixels must retain zero RGB contamination where transparency is supported. Before / After and Light Table remain honestly opaque in this candidate packet.

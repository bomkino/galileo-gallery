# The Shelf — S0 charter candidate

## Memorable outcome

Frames behave like collected editions sharing one physical shelf: each keeps its natural width, glides along a stable baseline with measured perspective, straightens and lifts for Spotlight, then returns to the exact shelf path. Recycling happens only beyond the shelf edge.

## Emotional register

Collected, tactile, editorial; a cared-for archive rather than a retail carousel.

## Motion sentence

Natural-width editions travel along one stable baseline, acquire restrained perspective through the reading zone, straighten and lift for focus, then rejoin the shelf before recycling fully offstage.

## Anti-goals

- no coverflow fan, generic horizontal carousel, bookstore mockup, 3D room, random shelf clutter, mouse-tilt gimmick, or free camera orbit;
- no source border, paper texture, shadow wash, lighting, tint, grain, blur, reflection, or opacity depth fade;
- no overlap collision, baseline drift, wrong-item lift, Spotlight snap, or Finale handoff reversal;
- no visible recycling or reindexing after failed media.

## Composition

One authored baseline sits below optical centre, leaving room for Spotlight lift and captions. Frame width follows source ratio inside a bounded common height. Perspective rotates around the vertical axis as items approach or leave the reading zone; the focused item straightens without changing source treatment. One item becomes a still collected edition. Two items maintain measured spacing. Recommended count is 5–24; bounded many is 127 with DOM windowing.

16:9 permits a wide travelling shelf. 9:16 reduces visible neighbours and increases vertical lift space. 1:1 and 4:5 preserve the baseline and caption zone. Canvas ratio remains distinct from per-frame contain/cover intent.

## Time

Automatic mode compiles one complete ordered traversal. Fixed duration changes velocity, not spacing or identity. Directed mode may include Spotlight and Finale phrases. Spotlight: selected edition approaches the reading zone, straightens, lifts, holds, lowers, and rejoins the same shelf path. Finale begins from the exact end of Spotlight release; it cannot drop focus, lift another item, or snap the selected edition upward. Reverse follows the same inverse geometry.

Casino rhythm may be compiled only if an approved Product Timeline asks for it and full traversal/seam topology survives. Reduced motion uses stable shelf tableaux and discrete focus changes.

## Controls

Causal controls: shelf height, edition height, gap, perspective, pace, Spotlight lift, Spotlight straighten, caption space, direction, fit, and background. Every control is bounded and resettable.

## Source and Look

Clean default preserves source RGB and alpha. Shelf material, shadows, or paper grain stay behind or around artwork if ever approved. Transparent output contains zero RGB beneath alpha zero. Failed media retain natural placeholder width, identity, caption, and order.

## Decision state

Candidate only. Human verdict pending. Not integrated, packaged, released, or accepted.

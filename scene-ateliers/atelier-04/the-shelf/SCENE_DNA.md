# The Shelf — Scene DNA

## Identity

An editorial baseline that respects natural edition widths. Its authored signature is the continuity between shelf travel, Spotlight straighten/lift, and exact rejoin.

## Spatial grammar

- one stable horizontal baseline;
- natural width from source ratio inside a common bounded height;
- measured yaw perspective through the reading zone;
- one caption/focus zone above the baseline;
- Spotlight lifts vertically and straightens around the edition’s centre;
- recycling occurs outside the visible shelf edge;
- fixed authored camera.

## Temporal grammar

`offstage entry → baseline glide → reading-zone perspective → Spotlight seek → straighten/lift → hold → lower/turn → exact path rejoin → offstage recycle`

Finale begins from the selected edition’s current state. It cannot substitute another identity or reverse vertical direction before continuing.

## Distinction

- Unlike Coverflow: no central fan or overlapping covers.
- Unlike Filmstrip River: natural widths and Spotlight collection semantics matter.
- Unlike Contact Strip: this is presentation, not a working review table.
- Unlike Deck River: one baseline replaces a depth corridor.

## Default taste

Common edition height 42% of short stage axis; 40 design-pixel gap at 1080; moderate 18° yaw; calm traversal; Spotlight lift 11% of stage height; contain fit; captions optional but structurally reserved.

## Deletion test

Remove natural widths and it becomes a generic strip. Remove the stable baseline and it becomes Scatter. Remove exact Spotlight rejoin and the Scene loses physical credibility. All three are essential.

2. Is it clearly distinct from Calm Ring, Proximity Orbit, Wide Ellipse, and a generic 3D carousel?
3. Does the silhouette remain recognisable when colour, labels, shadows, and textures are ignored?

### Pace

4. Does the canonical 12-second cycle feel precise rather than sleepy?
5. Are `[3,-2,1]` revolutions legible, or does the inner ring become frantic?

### Readability

7. Does the primary remain fully readable while satellites cross its plane?

### Physical continuity

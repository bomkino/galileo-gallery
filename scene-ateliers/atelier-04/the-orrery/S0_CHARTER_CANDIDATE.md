# The Orrery — S0 charter candidate

## Gate state

**G10B preflight candidate; implementation blocked by G10A.** This packet may refine identity, evaluator, renderer comparison, controls, risks, and review questions. It does not activate Product implementation.

## Memorable outcome

A stable primary frame anchors a small slide solar system. Secondary frames travel on distinct nested orbital planes. At authored intervals, the next body approaches the centre, inherits primary status without losing identity, and the former primary returns to its own ring.

## Emotional register

Precise wonder; quiet cosmology; a composed information instrument rather than a screensaver.

## Motion sentence

Nested rings turn at coherent integer-period rates around a stable primary while identity-preserving exchanges hand the centre from one frame to the next without scale, position, or source-treatment discontinuity.

## Anti-goals

- no generic orbit carousel with extra rings;
- no random planetary speed, free camera orbit, mouse gravity, lens flare, glow, starfield spectacle, or simulated lighting over artwork;
- no teleporting primary replacement, crossfade, identity swap, or size pop;
- no ring that fails to close at the story loop;
- no source tint, grain, blur, border, reflection, or post-processing.

## Composition

One primary plane occupies the optical centre. Three nested orbital planes remain readable as a system but subordinate to the primary through scale and geometry—not artwork dimming. Ring membership is stable by media identity. One item becomes a still primary. Two items form primary plus one measured satellite. Recommended count is 9–21; bounded many is 72.

Canvas policy adapts ring ellipticity and radius while preserving primary scale. 9:16 stacks rings vertically; 16:9 allows wider orbital ellipses; 1:1 and 4:5 keep a generous centre. Per-frame fit stays independent.

## Time

Every ring completes an integer number of signed revolutions per full Scene loop. The exchange index advances through every media identity before the loop closes. At an exchange boundary, the incoming body’s centre position and scale exactly equal the new primary state, while the outgoing body exactly rejoins its ring state. Automatic, fixed-duration, directed, reverse, Spotlight, and reduced-motion compilation remain deterministic.

Casino rhythm is not forced. If later approved, Product-directed fast ×2, regular ×1, fast ×1 must compile to complete ring/exchange topology rather than truncate a solar cycle.

## Renderer decision

DOM/CSS 3D remains the canonical candidate. A raw WebGL2 instanced-atlas probe consumes the same evaluator for preflight comparison of many-body spatial pressure. No renderer promotion or Product integration occurs while G10A blocks G10B.

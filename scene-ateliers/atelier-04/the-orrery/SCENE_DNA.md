# Scene DNA — The Orrery

Status: **G10B preflight candidate; implementation blocked by G10A**
Verdict: **pending**

## Silhouette-only fingerprint

One dominant rectangular primary at centre. Smaller rectangles occupy two or three differently tilted, differently compressed orbital planes around it. Some satellites sit behind the primary; some cross in front. The silhouette is hierarchical rather than evenly radial.

Reject the still if:

- all frames share one ring;
- the centre frame is merely the closest carousel card;
- concentric guide lines carry the identity instead of source planes;
- satellites appear randomly scattered;
- every satellite has equal projected depth/scale;
- primary identity is ambiguous.

## Five-second motion fingerprint

- inner and outer memberships remain stable;
- at least two planes move in opposite directions;
- revolution rates are visibly related, not random;
- satellites pass behind and in front of the primary;
- the primary remains still and readable in automatic mode;
- no cumulative seam drift appears;
- a directed exchange, when present, swaps primary/satellite roles through continuous paired paths.

Remove colour, guides, shadows, labels, and textures. The crop must still read as a nested primary/satellite apparatus.

## Material register

Precision physical model. Media planes behave like pieces mounted on an authored mechanism, not planets with decorative glow. Restraint matters: hierarchy, occlusion, and repeatable motion do the work.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. Primary comes from serialized intent, never index zero or local click state.
2. Ring membership is deterministic and stable.
3. Signed integer revolution counts reconcile exactly at the loop seam.
4. Role exchange preserves the target’s vacated hierarchy.
5. Artwork remains opacity `1`, filter `none`, normal blend.
6. Guides and lighting are optional/off or around artwork.
7. Reduced motion preserves hierarchy.
8. Production remains blocked by G10A.

## Adjacent-Scene separation

| Adjacent Scene | Orrery distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Quiet Carousel | No linear track or focus well; centre is serialized primary authority. |
| Calm Ring | Multiple projected planes, hierarchy, alternating signed revolutions. |
| Proximity Orbit | Not one camera-proximity ellipse; membership and primary roles are explicit. |
| Wide Ellipse | Not a broad single plane; exact nested plane reconciliation. |
| Zoetrope | No fast cylindrical gate cadence; Orrery is slower hierarchical mechanics. |
| Spiral Vortex | No single continuous thread or endpoint seam; satellites remain ring members. |

## Failure verdict

If the default cannot survive without orbit guides, if automatic mode changes primary, if exchange teleports identities, or if the motion reads as a generic orbit preset, recommend removal or redesign. Do not preserve the catalogue count by hiding the difference in constants.

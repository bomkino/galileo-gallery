# Spiral Image Vortex — Scene DNA

## Identity

One authored helix with honest front/back crossings. Its identity depends on continuous spatial travel and invisible offstage recycling—not spin, camera movement, or post-processing.

## Spatial grammar

- vertical helical centreline;
- fixed camera and stable horizon;
- central negative-space well;
- frame planes remain approximately camera-readable at the front passage;
- depth ordering follows geometry;
- wrap zone exists beyond the top/bottom visible envelope.

## Temporal grammar

`enter rear lane → climb/descend helix → front passage → recede → offstage wrap → equivalent re-entry`

Spotlight: `shortest front-passage seek → settle → hold → inverse release`. The selected identity cannot switch to a rear object because its angular projection is numerically similar.

## Distinction

- Unlike Orbit: vertical travel and helix pitch are essential.
- Unlike Zoetrope: no front gate or ratcheted holds.
- Unlike Deck River: path wraps around an axis instead of approaching through a corridor.
- Unlike Orrery: all frames share one continuous path rather than independent rings.

## Default taste

Radius 2.3 world units; 2.75 visible turns; pitch 6.2; 24 representative frames; restrained plane scale; contain fit; transparent-capable clean world.

## Deletion test

Remove front/back crossing and it becomes a vertical ribbon. Remove vertical pitch and it becomes Orbit. Remove offstage wrap and the loop visibly breaks. All three are essential.

# Spiral Image Vortex — S0 charter candidate

## Memorable outcome

Frames occupy one continuous helical path through space. Each image rises, crosses the front reading zone, recedes behind the axis, and recycles only after it is fully outside the visible world.

## Emotional register

Cinematic pull; controlled vertigo; a spatial procession rather than a decorative spiral.

## Motion sentence

Ordered frames travel along one unbroken helix, passing naturally in front and behind while the fixed camera watches a coherent stream with no visible teleport.

## Anti-goals

- no flat spiral pasted onto the screen;
- no random vortex noise, free orbit camera, mouse-parallax gimmick, or centre suction effect;
- no rear-facing Spotlight selection;
- no hard reset at the loop seam;
- no tint, light, blur, grain, border, depth fade, or material effect applied to source artwork.

## Composition

The helix is authored around a vertical axis with a generous central void. Frame identities remain stable through front/back crossings. Occlusion comes from geometric depth and stable z-order, not opacity treatment. One item becomes a single restrained vertical breath. Two items share opposing helix phases. Recommended count is 12–28; bounded many is 96 through windowed DOM or an instanced WebGL comparison.

16:9 uses a wide helix with a visible rear lane. 9:16 increases pitch and narrows radius. 1:1 and 4:5 preserve a readable front passage. Canvas ratio never overwrites per-frame contain/cover intent.

## Time

The evaluator maps story time to one periodic phase. Automatic mode performs one topologically closed loop. Fixed duration changes phase rate only. Directed casino rhythm may compile two fast passes, one regular pass, and one fast finale while retaining the same helix and seam. Reverse follows the inverse path. Spotlight chooses the actual front passage, settles without a finite snap, holds, and releases along the inverse path.

Every modulo wrap must occur with the affected frame fully offstage and effectively invisible. Reduced motion uses a sequence of stable helix tableaux rather than continuous travel.

## Renderer decision

DOM/CSS 3D remains the canonical candidate. A dependency-free WebGL2 instanced-atlas probe consumes the same pure evaluator for honest many-item comparison. WebGL promotion requires human identity approval, real imported-media RGB/alpha proof, preview/scrub/export parity, context fallback, and bounded GPU memory.

## Decision state

Candidate only. Human verdict pending. Not integrated, packaged, released, or accepted.

# Vitrine — S0 charter candidate

## Memorable outcome

One precious frame occupies a quiet viewing chamber and receives a genuine still hold. Its successor then approaches along a reciprocal physical path, exchanges places, and settles. The Scene earns attention through stillness and object continuity—not cosmetic light.

## Emotional register

Intimate, reverent, patient; a museum pause without luxury-advertising gloss.

## Motion sentence

A single frame rests completely still, then physically exchanges with its successor along matched approach and departure paths before the chamber becomes still again.

## Anti-goals

- no slideshow cross-dissolve;
- no sheen, glass reflection, spotlight sweep, glow, bloom, blur, vignette, frame border, grain, simulated light, or source dimming;
- no continuous idle drift applied to artwork;
- no carousel queue visible around the primary;
- no abrupt selection snap or hard loop reset.

## Composition

The chamber contains one readable primary plane and at most one incoming/outgoing plane during exchange. Negative space is structural. The camera is fixed. One item remains a true still. Two items express the complete Scene. Recommended count is 2–12; bounded many is 24, queued outside the visible chamber.

16:9 and 4:5 permit a generous primary. 1:1 preserves an even chamber. 9:16 shifts the exchange path vertically to maintain scale. Canvas ratio remains independent from per-frame contain/cover intent.

## Time

`entry → still hold → reciprocal exchange → settle → still hold → … → exact seam`.

Automatic mode gives every item one complete hold/exchange. Fixed duration scales hold/exchange within readable minima. Directed mode supports authored holds and exchanges; casino rhythm is inappropriate by default and must not be imposed. Reverse traverses the same reciprocal paths. Spotlight seeks one media identity only at a stable phase boundary.

Reduced motion keeps the long still hold and performs a discrete identity replacement at the exchange boundary, with an announcement outside the stage. No fade substitutes for motion.

## Controls

Causal controls only: hold duration, exchange duration, approach distance, primary scale, exchange axis, direction, frame fit, and background inclusion. Every control is bounded and resettable.

## Source and Look

The chamber exists behind or around artwork. Clean default preserves source RGB and alpha. “Vitrine” does not authorise simulated glass or lighting. Transparent output contains zero RGB beneath alpha zero. Failed media retain identity and order.

## Decision state

Candidate only. Human verdict pending. Not integrated, packaged, released, or accepted.

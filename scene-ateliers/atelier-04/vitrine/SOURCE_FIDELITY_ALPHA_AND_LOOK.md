# Vitrine — source fidelity, alpha, and Look

## Clean source contract

Imported artwork renders at opacity 1 with normal blend and filter none. Vitrine’s name does not permit simulated glass, sheen, reflection, spotlight sweep, bloom, glow, vignette, blur, grain, border, drop-shadow wash, tint, desaturation, or source lighting. The chamber’s hierarchy comes from scale, placement, and negative space.

## Alpha

Transparent output removes the chamber world and clears to RGBA zero. Every fully transparent pixel has RGB zero. Soft artwork edges preserve correct alpha over black, white, red, blue, and checkerboard.

## Look boundary

A future Look may render a quiet deterministic world behind the frame. It cannot touch source pixels or create moving light over artwork. Grain, if approved later, is a separate decorrelated background pass and absent beneath alpha zero. Luminance remains stable during long holds and exchange.

## Media policy

`contain` is default. Explicit `cover` remains per-frame intent. Failed media keep their ID, queue position, hold, and exchange role. Source-video frames sample Product story time; Vitrine never owns audio or playback truth.

## Evidence required

Real imported images and video; decoded RGB comparison; transparent composites; hold/exchange boundary captures; failed media; one-item stillness; human confirmation that the chamber feels precious without cosmetic source treatment.

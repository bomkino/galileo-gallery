# Vitrine — Scene DNA

## Identity

The authored contrast is **genuine stillness versus one reciprocal object exchange**. Vitrine is not a lighting effect and not a generic slideshow.

## Spatial grammar

- one fixed chamber;
- one primary frame;
- at most one exchange partner visible;
- matched incoming/outgoing paths;
- stable camera and generous negative space;
- no surrounding carousel inventory.

## Temporal grammar

`settled still → long hold → incoming approach / outgoing departure → exact handoff → settle → long hold`

Velocity is exactly zero during holds. Position and scale are continuous at exchange boundaries. The incoming object reaches the exact primary transform; the outgoing object leaves from that same transform.

## Distinction

- Unlike CMS Slideshow: no crossfade and no autoplay strip.
- Unlike Coverflow: no visible browsing queue or depth fan.
- Unlike Hero Deck Object: no stack, peel, or card hierarchy.
- Unlike Light Table: one contemplative object, not a working review surface.

## Default taste

1,400 ms hold; 760 ms exchange; restrained 22% approach distance; contain fit; primary fills roughly 62% of the short stage axis; dark clean world; no idle artwork motion.

## Deletion test

Remove true stillness and Vitrine becomes a carousel. Remove reciprocal exchange and it becomes a slideshow. Add cosmetic light and source treatment becomes the identity instead of the object. Stillness and exchange are essential; gloss is forbidden.

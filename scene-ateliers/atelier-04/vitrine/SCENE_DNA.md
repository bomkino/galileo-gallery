# Scene DNA — Vitrine

## Silhouette-only fingerprint

One isolated rectangle occupies a composed field of negative space, slightly above optical centre. Optional placard sits outside and below. No row, ring, stack, grid, or persistent second object is visible during the defining hold.

Reject the still if:

- more than one object competes during a hold;
- a reflection pool or glow carries the composition;
- the source fills the canvas like a generic slideshow;
- negative space feels accidental rather than measured;
- the caption overlays artwork;
- object presence depends on tinting or filtering source pixels.

## Five-second motion fingerprint

Most of the crop is still. When exchange begins:

- outgoing and incoming are the only source planes;
- outgoing travels toward one side and incoming arrives from the opposite side;
- both keep opacity `1`;
- bounded yaw/depth communicates object passage;
- no camera zoom, crossfade, light sweep, or sway occurs;
- incoming settles exactly at the same presentation station.

A five-second crop that shows constant movement has failed the Scene.

## Material register

Quiet gallery viewing room. Object edge, paper surround, plinth, or shadow may suggest material presence around the work, but the source remains untouched. Restraint is structural, not “luxury” styling.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. One readable object during holds.
2. Maximum two source planes during exchange.
3. Source opacity `1`, filter `none`, normal blend for every interval.
4. No continuous hold motion.
5. Exchange is reversible and geometric, not opacity-led.
6. Reflection/sheen absent by default.
7. Negative space survives all supported canvases.
8. Placard stays outside artwork.

## Adjacent-Scene separation

| Adjacent Scene | Vitrine distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Quiet Carousel | No continuous track or focus well; holds are one-object stillness. |
| Coverflow | No persistent neighbours or depth browsing row. |
| Contact Table | Not a scanning surface; only one work receives attention. |
| Scatter | No multi-print table or ambient drift. |
| Shelf | No shared baseline or collected row. |
| Opening Reel | No river/roll; change is a bounded object exchange. |

## Failure verdict

If the default needs crossfade, glow, reflection, source light, or constant sway to feel alive, reject or redesign it. If the exchange becomes a generic slide transition with no object presence, consolidate rather than protect catalogue count.

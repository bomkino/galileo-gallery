# Resource and lifecycle notes — Vitrine

## Observed laboratory bounds

- complete identity records: `N`;
- stage source nodes: maximum `2` across `202` sampled loop/finite timestamps;
- placard nodes: maximum `1`;
- source-treatment deviations: `0`;
- transport: one requestAnimationFrame only while Play is active;
- network requests, real video decoders, WebGL contexts, reflection buffers, light animations, and shared Product imports: `0`.

## Required Product disposal

Cancel transport, disconnect resize observation, remove listeners, pause/release current/incoming video resources, revoke source authority through Product media services, and reconstruct state from serialized config + ordered media + story time after remount.

## Evidence limitation

System Chromium captures, generated SVGs, deterministic readback, and alpha composites prove laboratory mechanics. They do not prove Product save/open/export, encoded alpha, external source-pixel equality, target-platform performance, long-running heap behavior, screen-reader behavior, or human acceptance.

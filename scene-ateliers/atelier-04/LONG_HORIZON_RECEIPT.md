## Hard rules retained

- Pure deterministic story-time remains renderer-independent.
- Preview, scrub, and later export must sample the same semantic evaluator.
- WebGL does not own the camera merely because it is WebGL.
- Imported artwork is not tinted, lit, blurred, grained, bordered, or post-processed by default.
- Live gestures are transient; one completed gesture produces one commit.
- GPU resources, observers, listeners, animation frames, and context restoration are explicit lifecycle obligations.
- Automated evidence proves mechanics only. Human taste remains pending.

No Product integration, PR, merge, package, release, deployment, or human acceptance is claimed.

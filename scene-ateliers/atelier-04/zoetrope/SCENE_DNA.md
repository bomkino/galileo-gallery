# Zoetrope — Scene DNA

## Identity

A reading machine, not an orbit. Its authored signature is the contrast between a short mechanical advance and a calm front-gate hold.

## Spatial grammar

- one cylinder;
- one stable front gate;
- coherent frame order around the circumference;
- rear geometry supplies depth but never modifies artwork pixels;
- camera fixed to the apparatus.

## Temporal grammar

`rest → accelerate → ratchet advance → settle → gate hold → repeat → complete-revolution seam`

No phase may begin from a state different from the prior phase’s end. Spotlight selects the shortest signed angular path and releases along its inverse.

## Distinction

- Unlike Orbit: attention comes from a viewing gate, not a front-most orbital body.
- Unlike Orrery: all frames share one radius and one cadence.
- Unlike Vortex: no helix, vertical travel, or front/back crossing.
- Unlike Shelf: no horizontal editorial baseline.

## Default taste

Moderate radius; 10 frames; 420 ms advance; 700 ms hold; restrained perspective; contain fit; clean charcoal world. The default must read at real speed before any control is touched.

## Deletion test

Remove the gate hold and Zoetrope collapses into generic orbit. Remove the cylinder and it collapses into slideshow. Remove the ratchet and it loses apparatus character. All three remain essential.

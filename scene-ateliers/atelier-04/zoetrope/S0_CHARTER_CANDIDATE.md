# Zoetrope — S0 charter candidate

## Memorable outcome

A ring of authored frames turns like a physical zoetrope, but the viewer reads one stable front gate at a time. Motion feels ratcheted and mechanical, not like a generic carousel.

## Emotional register

Playful apparatus; measured anticipation; a small machine revealing images through cadence.

## Motion sentence

The cylinder accelerates into a short turn, settles one frame into the viewing gate, holds long enough to read, then advances again without losing frame identity.

## Anti-goals

- no continuous orbit pretending to be a zoetrope;
- no camera orbit, free drag, roulette spin, random wobble, or source-frame crossfade;
- no light sweep, tint, blur, grain, frame border, or material treatment over artwork;
- no wall-clock evaluator or hard loop reset.

## Composition

The world is one shallow authored cylinder centred slightly below the optical middle. The viewing gate occupies the front third. Rear frames remain structurally legible but subordinate through geometric depth, never artwork dimming. A 1-item Project becomes a stationary drum. Two items alternate across opposite gates. Recommended count is 8–16; bounded many is 64 with DOM windowing.

Canvas behaviour: 16:9 and 4:5 use the full gate; 1:1 preserves top/bottom breathing room; 9:16 increases cylinder radius and lowers the horizon rather than shrinking artwork into illegibility. Per-frame contain/cover remains independent from canvas ratio.

## Time

Pure story-time evaluator. Automatic mode compiles one full revolution. Fixed-duration mode changes angular pace without changing gate order. Directed mode may compile fast ×2, regular ×1, fast ×1 as complete revolutions when Product intent requests casino rhythm. Spotlight mode has finite accelerate, settle, hold, and release phases. Reverse preserves the same physical path.

Loop seam occurs between equivalent complete-revolution states. No visible object teleports. Reduced motion replaces rotation with discrete gate changes separated by real holds; order and narrative remain intact.

## Controls

Only causal controls: cylinder radius, gate hold, advance duration, depth perspective, direction, frame fit, and background inclusion. Every control is bounded and has an explicit reset. Camera orbit is absent by charter.

## Source and Look

Clean default preserves source RGB and alpha. World treatment stays behind artwork. Transparent output contains zero RGB contamination beneath alpha zero. Failed media retain stable slots, names, and order.

## Decision state

Candidate only. Human verdict pending. Not integrated, packaged, released, or accepted.

## Required questions

### Identity

1. In silhouette, does this read immediately as a disciplined cylinder with a front gate—not Calm Ring, Wide Ellipse, Coverflow, or a horizontal carousel?
2. Does the apparatus feel optical/mechanical rather than decorative “3D”?
3. Is the shallow cylinder deep enough to establish tangency without turning side cards into useless slivers?

### Pace and cadence

4. At real speed, does the 68% travel / 32% dwell ratchet feel percussive and deliberate rather than frantic or stop-motion cheap?
5. Is 430 ms per slot the right default region? The alias check passes at 24/30/60 fps, but taste may still reject it.
6. Does `flywheel` add a genuinely useful second character, or should it be deleted to protect identity?

### Readability

7. Is the gate dwell long enough for typography at normal deck sizes?
8. Does the default six-item density give enough context without competing with the gate source?
9. In 9:16, does showing fewer/larger frames preserve the apparatus, or does the cylinder feel cramped?

### Physical continuity

10. Do cards remain convincingly tangent through side passage?
11. Is rear culling perceived as an occluded cylinder rather than a source pop?
12. Does reverse feel like the exact same machine running backwards?

### Source respect

13. Do generated alpha fixtures remain clean over black, white, red, blue, and checkerboard?
14. Is the absence of depth dimming preferable, or does geometry need a stronger around-artwork support plane from future Look?
15. Does any card edge, transform, or scale make the source feel altered rather than mounted?

### Defaults and consolidation

16. Keep recommended defaults: radius `0.39`, card size `0.31`, tilt `−4°`, ratchet, forward?
17. Is the Scene distinct enough to survive beside Calm Ring and Proximity Orbit, or should the catalogue consolidate it? Candidate recommendation: **keep**, because gate cadence and tangency form a separate grammar.

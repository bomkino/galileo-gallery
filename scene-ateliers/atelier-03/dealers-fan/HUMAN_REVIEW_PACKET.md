# Human review packet — Dealer's Pick

`verdict: pending`

## Review material

Use the canonical stills, five-second colour playback, five-second silhouette playback, portrait recomposition, alpha composites, exact readback, and test receipt under `evidence/`. Review at real speed before scrubbing.

## Identity

1. Does the silhouette read as one directional hand with a crown, not a symmetric Open Fan?
2. Is the below-stage pivot perceptible without becoming an obvious mechanical apparatus?
3. Does one card clearly own the reading moment while neighbours preserve ordered context?

## Pace and handoff

1. Can a viewer read the crown card before it yields?
2. Does fractional handoff feel continuous at the midpoint, with no snap or z-order theft?
3. Is the entry riffle useful material information, or decorative noise that should be deleted?
4. Does reverse feel like the exact physical action backward?

## Readability and physical continuity

1. Do mixed ratios remain anchored to one hand?
2. Do tall cards collide or obscure the crown in portrait?
3. Do active-window cards enter and leave without popping?
4. Does presentation lift feel like a card being offered rather than a generic scale-up?

## Defaults

1. `fan-step = 11°`: enough context without clutter?
2. `pivot-depth = 42%`: hand-shaped rather than wheel-shaped?
3. `visible-window = 7`: enough neighbour continuity for five and many-source cases?
4. `presentation-lift = 17%`: confident, not theatrical?
5. `card-size = 35%`: readable at wide and portrait canvases?

## Source respect and alpha

1. Is crown artwork visibly untouched by tint, dim, blur, or faux light?
2. Do transparent composites preserve clean edges over black, white, red, blue, and checkerboard?
3. Does failed media retain its rightful position without drawing disproportionate attention?

## Decision record required later

Record approve / revise / consolidate / reject, chosen default changes, any count/canvas exceptions, and whether the entry riffle survives. Automated checks cannot set this verdict.

# Scene DNA — Dealer's Pick

## Identity checksum

**Silhouette still:** one card is upright and high at a crown; neighbours descend in a directional arc toward a pivot visibly below the stage. The hand is not symmetric around a stable overview.

**Five-second crop:** the crown transfers continuously from one ordered card to the next. The outgoing card yields along the same radial path the incoming card used. No source pops, no z-order fight, no random flourish.

If either checksum fails, this is not Dealer's Pick.

## Non-negotiable traits

1. One stable pivot below stage.
2. One continuous fractional focus coordinate.
3. One readable crown gate.
4. Ordered ingress and egress through a bounded active window.
5. Exact temporal inverse for reverse.
6. Crown artwork at normal blend, opacity one, filter none.

## Geometry signature

- Bottom-centres describe a radial hand around a low pivot.
- Angular offset is proportional to source-index distance from focus.
- The crown card straightens because its offset approaches zero; it is not independently teleported upright.
- Presentation lift follows the crown arm, then returns to the same arm.
- Neighbours establish direction and continuity.
- Portrait recomposition makes the hand narrower and steeper, not merely smaller.

## Motion signature

- Fractional focus moves, cards do not swap slots.
- Handoff has three legible beats: approach, crown/read, yield.
- Ordinary traversal can pass without a hold; authored spotlight and finale compile real holds.
- Entry/exit collapse to the same hidden pivot pose.
- Reverse is the exact forward phrase sampled backward.

## Five-second read

At default five-card timing, a five-second crop must include at least one complete crown handoff and enough neighbouring motion to explain the mechanism. It must not spend the entire crop in a static fan, nor race so quickly that artwork becomes unreadable.

## Anti-DNA

- symmetric open overview;
- fixed fan with only one card scaled;
- card teleport to centre;
- scroll, hover, pointer, or rAF as output authority;
- random riffle timers;
- all cards fully opaque despite deep overlap;
- hidden reordering at the active-window boundary;
- shared Open Fan evaluator with constants renamed;
- generic orbit, coverflow, stack, or carousel grammar.

## Neighbour separation

| Neighbour | Decisive difference |
| --- | --- |
| Open Fan | Open Fan shows the collection simultaneously in stable slots; Dealer's Pick moves a focus through one crown. |
| Quiet Carousel | Quiet Carousel translates a linear track through a focus well; Dealer's Pick rotates a hand around a below-stage pivot. |
| Coverflow | Coverflow uses lateral front-plane browsing and side yaw; Dealer's Pick uses radial bottom-centre paths. |
| Calm Ring | Calm Ring loops every card around one orbital plane; Dealer's Pick has ingress/egress and one directional hand. |
| Zoetrope | Zoetrope is a cylinder apparatus with fast depth passage; Dealer's Pick remains a hand of camera-readable cards. |

## Deletion test

Delete Open Fan, every orbit study, and the central renderer. Dealer's Pick still has a complete identity because its evaluator requires fractional focus, crown geometry, low pivot, window ingress/egress, hold compilation, and exact inverse travel. Delete those properties and the Scene collapses into a generic fan preset.

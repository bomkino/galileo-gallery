# Human review packet — Open Fan

`verdict: pending`

## Review setup

Review the 16:9 five-card default, the mixed-ratio seven-card fixture, the two-card fixture, the 12-card limit, and 9:16 recomposition. Watch both colour and silhouette five-second clips at real speed. Do not judge from a single hero still.

## Identity

1. Does the silhouette read immediately as one hand opening from a common hinge?
2. Does the stable overview dominate the identity, rather than the lifted card?
3. Is it unmistakably different from Dealer's Pick's moving crown selection?

## Pace

1. Does entry feel resistant and paper-like without becoming slow ceremony?
2. Is the overview held long enough to read the whole collection?
3. Does the inverse close feel causally connected to entry?

## Readability

1. Are the outer cards readable enough at the default 135° spread?
2. Do portrait cards feel intentionally taller rather than colliding?
3. Is the 9:16 derived spread composed rather than cramped?

## Physical continuity

1. Do all bottom centres convincingly share one hinge?
2. Does the spotlight card rise along its existing slot and return exactly?
3. Does any neighbour appear to reorder, jump, or steal depth during lift?

## Defaults

1. Choose between `paper-breath = 0` and `0.35%` after viewing both at real speed.
2. Confirm or revise 135° spread, 79% hinge height, 36% card size, and 14% lift.
3. Confirm that a 12-card active window is honest enough for 13–127 sources.

## Source respect

1. At overview, spotlight hold, and finale hold, do source colours and contrast remain unchanged?
2. Do transparent edges composite cleanly over all evidence backgrounds?
3. Does failed media retain its slot and collection rhythm without drawing disproportionate attention?

## Decision record required later

Record approve/revise/consolidate, chosen defaults, acceptable count policy, reduced-motion preference, and any source-fidelity defect. Automated checks cannot supply this verdict.


## Authoring-review questions added after the open-source study

  its story source?
- Is the separate story-source label clear enough before export/save integration exists?
- Does direct card drag make temporal review faster without implying pointer-driven output?
- Are one-step undo/redo and 30 fps frame stepping sufficient for evaluating a Scene candidate?
- Does the inspection outline remain legible without contaminating artwork or the Scene silhouette?

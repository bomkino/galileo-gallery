# Resource and lifecycle observation — Open Fan

The pure evaluator computes identity and window selection for all ordered sources, then emits at most 12 mounted card poses. The ordinary five-source packet remains at five rendered cards across all sampled checkpoints; the 127-source check evaluates 127 ordered identities while mounting 12. No time-dependent collection grows.

The HTML preview uses `requestAnimationFrame` only as an authoring scheduler; scrub, test vectors, SVG evidence, and fixed-step playback pass explicit story time to the same pure evaluator. A Chromium headless attempt did not establish a functional page session in this runner and was terminated, so DOM focus traversal and browser remount are not claimed here.

The prototype creates no network request, persistent store, observer, worker, WebGL context, texture, or media decoder. Navigating away disposes document listeners by document teardown. Product remount/disposal and decoded-video budgets remain unintegrated and unclaimed.

The final prototype keeps source-ID-keyed DOM nodes stable, owns one single cancellable preview scheduler, pauses it when hidden, and exposes reduced motion/fixed duration/source inspection visibly. The evaluator remains stateless and reconstructible from config, ordered media, stage, and explicit story time.

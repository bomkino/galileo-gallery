# Scene DNA — Proximity Orbit

## Identity checksum

**Silhouette still:** one near card dominates through bounded physical scale and lower/closer position; far cards recede into a quiet, smaller upper arc. The shape is depth-emphatic, not a uniform ring or panoramic ellipse.

**Five-second crop:** far cards move briskly; approach visibly slows as one card swells nonlinearly into the near gate, holds, then yields. No light shift explains depth.

## Non-negotiables

1. Camera-distance path.
2. Nonlinear bounded near response.
3. Local near-time allocation distinct from far speed.
4. Collision-safe ratio-aware scale cap.
5. Near artwork normal / one / none.
6. Exact path/occlusion reverse.
7. Interaction excluded from output authority.

## Silhouette grammar

- one large near card;
- smaller far arc displaced vertically;
- shoulder cards explain continuous path;
- no uniform circle, broad cinematic shoulders, cylinder wall, or orbit line;
- portrait emphasizes depth rather than clipping sideways.

## Motion grammar

- strong but bounded scale derivative near camera;
- slower final approach/first departure;
- far recession through depth and alpha, never lighting;
- one authored near hold;
- reverse traverses exact same scale/position/occlusion states.

## Anti-DNA

- Calm Ring's uniform tray and restrained scale;
- Wide Ellipse's broad lateral panorama;
- Zoetrope's cylinder/tangential cards;
- Orrery's hierarchy/nested paths;
- hover speed-up/slow-down/pause;
- generic orbit pose plus larger scale constant.

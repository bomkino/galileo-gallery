# Source fidelity, alpha, and Look boundary — Proximity Orbit

## Near readable contract

At every declared near hold/readable passage: container opacity `1`; artwork opacity `1`; filter `none`; blend mode `normal`; no tint, blur, contrast, saturation, glow, grain, vignette, relight, reflection, or shader.

Far recession uses container geometry and alpha. It never changes source colour. Nonlinear nearness is position/scale/time, not illumination.

## Scene versus Look

Scene owns path, camera distance, vertical/depth projection, scale kernel/cap, container alpha, z-order, and bounded windowing. G10D Look owns background/world material behind/around cards. Look cannot react to near phase by washing source artwork.

Generated paper/colours are evidence fixtures only.

## Frame ownership

Frame/Product owns contain/cover, source ratio, padding, crop, focal point, and source box. Scene uses resolved outer dimensions for collision cap. It cannot crop or stretch a tall card to earn more swell.

## Video/failed media

Video uses Product story time and same path. Near approach never changes playback speed. Failed media retains source ID, fallback ratio, path phase, near turn, and accessible label. Late metadata recomposes only at a stable checkpoint.

## Alpha

Transparent mode emits no required orbit line, glow, light cone, shadow, or matte. Evidence includes source alpha and composites over black/white/red/blue/checkerboard. Deterministic sanitization guarantees zero RGB under zero alpha. Partial alpha is antialiasing only; geometry is identical across composites.

## Audio

No Scene audio control. Product owns all lanes, trims, loops, gain, ducking, mute/solo, master, and video audio.

## Unproved

External source RGB equivalence, Product colour management, encoded alpha, HDR/wide-gamut, final Look interaction, decoded-video resource behavior, and human verdict.

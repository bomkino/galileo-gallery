# Resource and lifecycle observation — Proximity Orbit

The ordinary seven-source study mounts seven; the 127-source study evaluates 127 ordered identities and mounts sixteen. Canonical checkpoint mount counts remain bounded and repeated rendering is hash-equal. The evaluator contains no hover, wall clock, timer, prior-frame, observer, pointer, or browser state.

The HTML preview may schedule explicit story time, but preview scheduling is not evaluator authority. The prototype owns no source decoder, network request, worker, WebGL context, texture, or persistent store. A Chromium headless attempt did not establish a functional page session in this runner and was terminated; browser DOM/focus/remount, heap stability, Product decode/disposal, and GPU context behavior remain unintegrated and unclaimed.

The final prototype keeps source-ID-keyed DOM nodes stable, owns one single cancellable preview scheduler, pauses it when hidden, and exposes reduced motion/fixed duration/source inspection visibly. The evaluator remains stateless and reconstructible from config, ordered media, stage, and explicit story time.

# Scene DNA — Wide Ellipse

## Identity checksum

**Silhouette still:** a broad, tilted panoramic arc spans most of the canvas. One front frame sits on the nearer shoulder; two readable shoulder frames anchor strong lateral breadth; rear frames complete a shallow receding arc. It is not a circle.

**Five-second crop:** frames make long lateral passes through approach shoulder, front gate, and departure shoulder. Scale changes geometrically and modestly; no card lunges, tumbles, or receives a light flourish.

## Non-negotiables

1. Independent major and minor ellipse dimensions.
2. One rotated plane with authored yaw and pitch.
3. Analytical depth/front gate and exact z-order.
4. Readable approach/front/departure zones.
5. Camera-facing source frames.
6. Geometric perspective only; no nonlinear proximity swell.
7. Exact loop/reverse and ordered identity.
8. No mandatory line, glow, apparatus, or tumble.

## Silhouette grammar

- path width materially exceeds path height in landscape;
- near side is spatially offset by plane yaw, not centred by fiat;
- both shoulders remain legible;
- rear arc is quieter but still explains continuity;
- portrait becomes taller/narrower through recomposition, never crop;
- no circle, cylinder wall, nested ring, central primary, or helix.

## Motion grammar

- long lateral displacement dominates;
- front/shoulder travel remains continuous and camera-readable;
- perspective scale follows one depth matrix;
- source planes stay broadly upright;
- spotlight and finale use ordinary path travel plus a hold;
- reverse traverses the exact same x/y/depth/alpha/order sequence.

## Anti-DNA

- Calm Ring's uniform circular tray;
- Proximity's nonlinear near swell and locally slowed near passage;
- Zoetrope's cylinder/tangential cards;
- Spiral Vortex's helix and card rotation;
- Orrery's primary/satellite hierarchy;
- generic orbit evaluator with width/height constants;
- individual card corkscrew or flourish timers.

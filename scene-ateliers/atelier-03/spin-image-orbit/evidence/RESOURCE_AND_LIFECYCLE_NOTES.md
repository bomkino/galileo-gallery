The pure evaluator reports every source identity as evaluated and no more than eighteen mounted cards for the 127-source fixture. Canonical ordinary fixtures mount their full count. Repeated SVG/PNG rendering is hash-equal. The prototype owns no source decoder, network request, observer, timer, worker, WebGL context, texture, or persistent store. A Chromium headless attempt did not establish a functional page session in this runner and was terminated. Browser DOM counts, focus traversal, heap stability, live remount, Product decode/disposal, and GPU/context-loss behavior remain unintegrated and unclaimed.

The evaluator remains stateless and reconstructible from config, ordered media, stage, and explicit story time.

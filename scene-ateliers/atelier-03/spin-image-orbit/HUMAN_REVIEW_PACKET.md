# Human review packet — Wide Ellipse

verdict: pending

## Review setup

Use generated six-source and mixed-ratio fixtures on 16:9 and 9:16. Watch the five-second color and silhouette clips at real speed, then scrub approach shoulder, front hold, later pass, finale, exit, and seam. Inspect black/white/red/blue/checkerboard alpha composites. Review reduced motion separately.

## Identity

1. Does a silhouette-only still read as a broad tilted panoramic ellipse rather than a stretched Calm Ring?
2. Does five seconds of motion read as lateral cinematic travel rather than Proximity's near/far swell?
3. Are the two shoulders and offset front gate memorable enough to justify a separate Scene?
4. Does the Scene remain recognisable with orbit line, glow, shadow, labels, and color removed?

## Pace

1. Is the approach shoulder long enough to anticipate the featured frame?
2. Does the front hold feel confident rather than stalled?
3. Is rear travel calm enough for a gallery but broad enough to feel cinematic?
4. Does reverse feel like the exact same physical path backward?
5. Does the seam disappear at real speed?

## Readability

1. Can both shoulder frames be read without competing with the front frame?
2. Are mixed portrait, square, wide, and narrow frames treated fairly?
3. Does the rear remain explanatory without tint or washed-out artwork?
4. Does portrait recomposition feel authored rather than cramped?
5. Is the front gate too far to one side at the 26° yaw default?

## Physical continuity

1. Do x/y/depth/scale/alpha agree through the whole plane rotation?
2. Does any card appear to tumble, corkscrew, pop, or steal z-order?
3. Does spotlight use the ordinary shoulder/front path and exact inverse departure?
4. Does finale arise from the ellipse rather than generic centre scale?
5. Do 2-source and bounded-many fixtures preserve ordered identity?

## Defaults and controls

1. Approve/reject default `88 / 34 / 26 / 14 / 21` for width/height/yaw/pitch/card size.
2. Are all five controls causally distinct and worth exposing?
3. Should yaw permit negative values, or would that duplicate Timeline direction in authors' mental model?
4. Does card size remain useful at both six and eighteen mounted sources?
5. Is geometric depth sufficient without a separate depth-strength control?

## Source respect

1. At front and shoulders, does artwork remain clearly untinted, unfiltered, normally blended, and unwashed?
2. Do transparent composites remain clean on every test ground?
3. Is rear container alpha acceptable without changing source color?
4. Do failed and video frames retain position/identity without special motion?
5. Does reduced motion still communicate Wide Ellipse without animating?

## Acceptance record

Human reviewer, date, decision, requested changes, and evidence references remain blank until an actual review occurs. Automated checks cannot fill this section.

# Scene DNA — Calm Ring

## Identity checksum

**Silhouette still:** one evenly spaced projected ring; one clear front card; rear cards smaller and occluded in the same plane. No second ring, no wide panoramic arc, no cylinder wall.

**Five-second crop:** the entire ordered plane turns coherently. Front passage, shoulder passage, rear recession, and occlusion agree. Motion is calm enough to read one front artwork.

## Non-negotiables

1. One circular plane.
2. Equal ordered angular spacing.
3. One front gate.
4. Position/depth/scale/alpha/z-order derived from one projection.
5. Camera-readable cards.
6. Exact loop and reverse.
7. Front artwork normal / one / none.

## Silhouette grammar

- compact projected circle, not broad ellipse;
- front card centred near lower-middle/centre;
- shoulders balanced left/right;
- rear cards visibly recede behind;
- no line, globe, glow, hub, satellite, or ring decoration required.

## Five-second motion grammar

- one coherent tray rotation;
- no nonlinear speed burst at camera;
- no card self-spin;
- no hover pause or momentum in output;
- spotlight becomes a true phase hold;
- reverse retraces identical poses and occlusion.

## Anti-DNA

- Proximity's nonlinear near swell;
- Wide Ellipse's panoramic lateral sweep;
- Zoetrope's fast cylinder/apparatus;
- Spiral Vortex's helix;
- Orrery's primary/satellites/nested planes;
- Quiet Carousel's linear track;
- generic orbit helper with preset constants.

## Neighbour proof

| Scene | What Calm Ring refuses |
| --- | --- |
| Proximity Orbit | camera-distance drama and nonlinear swell |
| Wide Ellipse | independent width/height and strong lateral shoulders |
| Zoetrope | tangential card walls and rapid cylindrical rhythm |
| Orrery | hierarchy, primary body, nested rings |
| Spiral Vortex | changing radius/height along a helix |

## Deletion test

Calm Ring survives deletion of every other orbit because it owns the minimal coherent tray: equal spacing, one plane, one front gate, geometry-derived falloff, and calm phase travel. If any second hierarchy or nonlinear near behavior is required, the design belongs elsewhere.

# Resource and lifecycle observation — Calm Ring

The evaluator maps all ordered identities to one stable circular plane and emits no historical motion state. Ordinary six-source samples mount six cards; the 127-source study evaluates 127 identities and mounts eighteen. Canonical checkpoint mount counts remain bounded and repeated rendering is hash-equal.

Preview scheduling is not evaluator authority. The prototype owns no source decoder, network request, timer, observer, worker, WebGL context, texture, or persistent store. A Chromium headless attempt did not establish a functional page session in this runner and was terminated, so browser DOM counts, focus traversal, remount, heap stability, Product decode, disposal, and GPU context behavior remain unintegrated and unclaimed.

The final prototype keeps source-ID-keyed DOM nodes stable, owns one single cancellable preview scheduler, pauses it when hidden, and exposes reduced motion/fixed duration/source inspection visibly. The evaluator remains stateless and reconstructible from config, ordered media, stage, and explicit story time.

# Source fidelity, alpha, and Look boundary — Calm Ring

## Readable source state

At the front gate and declared readable shoulder zone:

- artwork opacity `1`;
- filter `none`;
- blend mode `normal`;
- no tint, brightness shift, saturation shift, blur, glow, grain, vignette, or shader;
- no Scene-authored reflection or lighting pass.

Rear recession uses the card container's geometry and alpha. It never rewrites source colour, and the front container is exactly opaque.

## Scene versus Look

Scene owns circular tray coordinates, projection tilt, centre, perspective scale, container alpha, occlusion, and z-order. G10D Look owns world/background material and decorrelated treatment behind/around cards. No ring line, star field, halo, or glow is required for identity.

Generated prototype paper/colour is fixture-only.

## Frame ownership

Frame/Product owns contain/cover, crop, focal point, ratio override, padding, corner treatment, and decoded source box. Scene consumes resolved outer bounds and may reduce effective card width for collision. It never changes ratio or crop to make the ring regular.

## Video and failures

Images/videos share one evaluator. Product story time controls video frame. Preview may suspend rear decoders while retaining target time. Failed media retains ID, ratio fallback, angular slot, front passage, and accessible name.

## Alpha

Transparent mode emits no world fill and no mandatory line/glow/shadow. Evidence includes transparent source plus black/white/red/blue/checkerboard composites. After deterministic sanitization, every zero-alpha pixel must contain zero RGB; partial alpha is limited to antialiased edges. Geometry must match across composites.

## Audio

No Scene audio parameter or behavior. Product lanes own source-video audio, presenter, soundtrack, trims, loop, gain, ducking, mute, and master.

## Not proved by atelier

External source RGB parity, Product colour management, encoded alpha codecs, HDR/wide gamut, final Look interaction, and human source-respect verdict remain unclaimed.

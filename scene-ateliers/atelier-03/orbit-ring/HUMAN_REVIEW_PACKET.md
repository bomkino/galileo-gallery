# Human review packet — Calm Ring

`verdict: pending`

Review real-speed colour and silhouette playback before scrubbing.

## Identity

1. Does one compact, quiet tray read immediately?
2. Is the front gate unambiguous without a ring line or glow?
3. Is it clearly distinct from Proximity, Wide Ellipse, Zoetrope, Spiral Vortex, and Orrery?

## Pace and continuity

1. Can front artwork be read at default pace?
2. Do y, scale, alpha, depth, and occlusion agree through shoulders/rear?
3. Does reverse retrace the same physical ring?
4. Does spotlight settle without a hidden snap?

## Defaults

1. `ring-radius = 38%`: compact but not crowded?
2. `plane-tilt = 12°`: enough depth without becoming an ellipse spectacle?
3. `card-size = 24%`: readable without collisions?
4. Is the rear alpha floor calm, or visually muddy?

## Edge/source respect

1. Do 1/2-card cases remain honest?
2. Does portrait recompose rather than clip?
3. Does 18-source virtualization still read as one ring?
4. Is front artwork visibly untouched?
5. Are transparent edges clean across all composites?

Record approve / revise / consolidate / reject and final defaults. Automated checks cannot set the verdict.


## Authoring-review questions added after the open-source study

- Does keyboard inspection feel like looking around the authored Scene rather than silently editing
  its story source?
- Is the separate story-source label clear enough before export/save integration exists?
- Does direct card drag make temporal review faster without implying pointer-driven output?
- Are one-step undo/redo and 30 fps frame stepping sufficient for evaluating a Scene candidate?
- Does the inspection outline remain legible without contaminating artwork or the Scene silhouette?

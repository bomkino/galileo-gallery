# Edge, resource, and accessibility policy — Quiet Carousel

## Edge behavior

- 0: honest empty stage; no fabricated cards.
- 1: one source breathes 2.5% on the major axis; reduced motion makes it still.
- 2: two stable identities share a closed track. Sparse negative space may expand beyond the requested minimum gap.
- 3–12: preferred authored range.
- 13–256: evaluator remains bounded; later renderer mounts visible frames plus overscan only.
- Mixed ratios keep source dimensions and order. Failed media retains its slot. Video time is global story time.

## Accessibility and reviewer UX

The isolated workbench exposes every Scene control with a label and numeric output, named pose buttons, Reset, exact scrub, direction, canvas, silhouette, composites, count and reduced motion. The visible animated status is not live. One hidden throttled live region announces discrete actions only. Play exposes `aria-pressed`; OS reduced-motion preference initializes the control; enabling reduced motion pauses transport.

Moving artwork is not focusable. Canvas has an honest dynamic image label. Product source selection and keyboard navigation remain outside the Scene.

## Resource/lifecycle

Pure evaluator: one state object per source. No wall clock or persistent cache. Later renderer must cancel animation frames, disconnect observers, pause off-window video, revoke object URLs and dispose contexts on unmount/context loss. Browser evidence includes one full-phrase bounded observation, not a heap-soak verdict.

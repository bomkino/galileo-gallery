# Scene DNA — Two-lane Filmstrip

## Irreducible identity

Two parallel material ribbons. Equal authority. Opposite travel. Stable source spacing. Invisible offstage recycling.

## Silhouette test

A still must show two separated, parallel bands of upright frames. Real-speed playback must show the bands moving against one another without card-level wobble. Remove source colour and it must not resemble Quiet Carousel's single focus well, Wave's curved path, or either Deck River corridor.

## Physical laws

1. Lane count is two.
2. Lane speed magnitudes match.
3. Lane direction signs oppose.
4. Lane baselines never drift.
5. Source order within each parity stream is stable.
6. No frame changes luminance, opacity, rotation, or scale to simulate focus.
7. Recycle only beyond the visible guard band.

## Deletion test

Delete dirt, sprockets, captions, shadows, grain, and colour: identity survives. Delete either lane or counter-flow: identity dies.

## Adjacent-scene boundary

- Quiet Carousel: one composed focus lane; this Scene has two equal material lanes.
- Wave: one curved path; this Scene has two straight baselines.
- Deck River: perspective corridor; this Scene is orthographic and depthless.
- Contact strip: review/editing layout may carry labels or selection semantics; this Scene is continuous motion only.

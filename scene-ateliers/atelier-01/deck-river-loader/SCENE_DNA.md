# Scene DNA — Chapter Reveal

## Irreducible identity

A corridor **resolves** into one chapter arrival. The finite resolution—not merely corridor depth—is the identity.

## Silhouette and playback test

Early still: converging depth corridor. Arrival still: one centred target, other frames physically cleared. Playback: visible transfer of momentum from corridor to straightening/growth, followed by hold and forward takeover. It must never read as continuous Deck River paused at a convenient frame.

## Physical laws

1. One finite phrase; no loop.
2. Target identity comes from Product intent.
3. Acquire pose is the actual corridor pose.
4. Arrival begins with inherited position derivative and ends at zero velocity.
5. Non-targets clear through corridor geometry, not opacity fades.
6. Hold freezes visual geometry, not source-video time or audio.
7. Exit continues forward into takeover; no teleport.

## Deletion test

Delete background, colour, captions, and Look: corridor-to-centred-arrival silhouette survives. Delete target resolution: Scene collapses into Continuous Deck River and must be consolidated.

## Adjacent-scene boundary

- Continuous Deck River: closed endless corridor; Chapter Reveal is open, finite, and resolves.
- Opening Reel: ceremonial sequence with authored pauses; Chapter Reveal is one momentum-driven spatial threshold.
- Quiet Carousel: calm closed focus travel; Chapter Reveal accelerates and terminates.

Look owns corridor room, ground, atmosphere, stable luminance, deterministic background motion, and any transition into the next Scene. The clean Scene may output transparent pixels.

A soft-alpha target remains soft alpha during arrival and takeover. The Scene cannot silently paint a backing rectangle to force a wipe. Future encoded evidence must verify zero RGB beneath alpha zero; generated composites here test composition only.

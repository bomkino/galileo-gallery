# Scene DNA — Ribbon Wave

## Irreducible identity

One continuous curved path. The path is fixed. Frames move through it. Smooth crest, smooth trough, exact seam.

## Silhouette test

A still must read as one sinusoidal chain, not two lanes or a straight strip. Playback must show cards advancing through stationary crests and troughs. Remove colour and labels: the curve survives.

## Physical laws

1. One path only.
2. No per-card phase noise.
3. Path position and derivative close at seam.
4. Source order remains stable.
5. Tangent influence is partial and capped.
6. Crest focus is a path landmark, not scale or luminance treatment.
7. No depth, lighting, or source opacity hierarchy.

## Deletion test

Delete colour, background, rotation, and Look: curved positional silhouette survives. Delete the curve or let cards bob independently: identity dies.

## Adjacent-scene boundary

- Filmstrip: two straight counter-running lanes; Wave is one curved path.
- Quiet Carousel: one straight focus well with depth falloff; Wave has repeated crest/trough geometry without depth.
- Deck River: camera corridor and perspective; Wave is orthographic.

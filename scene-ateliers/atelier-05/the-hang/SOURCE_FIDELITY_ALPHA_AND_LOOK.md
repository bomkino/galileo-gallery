# Source fidelity, alpha, and Look — The Hang

## Source contract

Artwork remains opaque, unfiltered, and normally blended. Pendulum transforms the frame object; it never applies source brightness, blur, tint, dimming, glow, or grading. Focus is wire restraint plus external registration, not photometric treatment.

## Legitimate Scene pixels

- pivot/rail hardware;
- wire;
- physical frame/paper edge;
- source-safe focus registration.

Debug arcs and labels are evidence-only. Background, lighting, grain, vignette, and global paper palette remain Look responsibilities.

## Alpha

Transparent output is supported. Representative 960×540 evidence reports:

- 496,946 zero-alpha pixels;
- 0 hidden-RGB contaminated zero-alpha pixels;
- 0 partial-alpha pixels in the hard-edge raster;
- 21,454 opaque apparatus/artwork pixels.

Black, white, red, blue, and checkerboard composites are generated. Product browser/GPU/export alpha remains unproven until S1 repeats the test.

## Failed/video media

A failed source keeps its exact frame, wire, pivot, length, index, and focus role. Video requested time is deterministic story time; decoding and audio remain external.

## Forbidden treatments

No spotlight brightness, neighbour dimming, rail glow, drop-shadow wash across artwork, motion blur contamination, grain-through-alpha, or scene-owned background.

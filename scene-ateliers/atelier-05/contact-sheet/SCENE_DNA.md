# Scene DNA — Contact Sheet

## Irreducible identity

**One opaque whole-set sheet + ordered non-overlapping grid + moving editorial registration marks.** Remove the whole-set view, opaque sheet, stable cells, or proofing mark and the Scene becomes Focus Strip, Light Table, or a generic gallery grid.

## Memorable still

Every accepted frame is visible on one continuous paper/contact surface in predictable source order. Equal cells, keylines, indices/captions, and one restrained registration bracket make the selected frame legible without enlarging it.

## Memorable movement

Only the registration brackets move. They align to one cell, travel through authored reading order, expand to frame the entire grid, hold the whole-set finale, and contract to source one. Source cells never move, scale, lift, fade, reorder, or glow.

## Topology

- Ordered non-overlapping rows and columns.
- Source order fixed in cell topology.
- Last partial row centred without reordering.
- One/two deliberate sparse layouts.
- Maximum 24; no pagination.
- Focus traversal independent from cell placement.
- Opaque continuous sheet.

## Material logic

The paper/contact surface and cell keylines make the set read as one proof object. Registration brackets act as editorial annotation. Illumination, loose prints, lightbox, shadow theatre, texture, grain, and scene-owned colour choices are excluded.

## Temporal signature

`cell hold → mark travel`, repeated across the set, then `last cell → whole-set frame → whole-set hold → first cell`. Grid topology is invariant at every sample. Reverse is exact story-time retrace.

## Source roles

Every source is an equal ordered cell. Selection is a mark relation, not a source transformation. Failed sources remain equal cells. The whole-set finale privileges sequence structure, not the final image.

## Source-count signatures

- 1: one large proof cell on one sheet.
- 2: balanced two-cell sheet.
- 8–16: primary editorial survey.
- 17–24: bounded dense survey; all frames remain visible.
- >24: explicit unsupported boundary, not hidden pagination.

## Essential controls

Columns, gutter, sheet margin, traversal, labels. Columns/gutter/margin recompile layout; traversal/labels do not.

## Neighbour exclusions

### Versus Focus Strip

Focus Strip moves a local ordered sequence beneath one fixed station. Contact Sheet keeps the whole set visible and stationary; the mark moves instead. Focus Strip has an offstage track and scan stops. Contact Sheet has no offstage space, recycling, or moving sources.

### Versus Light Table

Light Table may use illumination, loose review-room arrangement, overlapping material, or a loupe. Contact Sheet uses one opaque non-illuminated sheet, strict non-overlapping order, and registration marks. No glow, source brightness, loose tilt, or overlap enters this charter.

### Versus generic grid

A generic grid may simply sit. Contact Sheet owns editorial traversal, indices/captions, proofing marks, whole-set finale, opaque paper material, and an explicit no-pagination capability boundary.

## Delete-to-test

- Delete opaque sheet: identity becomes a floating grid.
- Move or scale cells during focus: identity collapses toward card gallery.
- Hide most cells: identity collapses toward Focus Strip.
- Add illumination/glow/overlap: identity collapses toward Light Table.
- Reorder to fill the last row: editorial trust fails.

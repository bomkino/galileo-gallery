# Scene DNA — Lively Prints

## Memorable outcome

A burst of ordered prints arrives from the perimeter, resolves around a deliberate hole in the composition, trades short physical attention, and leaves on the same owned routes.

## Irreducible silhouette signature

Still:

- distributed field rather than compact overlap cluster;
- visible intentional negative space;
- mixed ratios with clear individual boundaries;
- no grid, lane, orbit, pile, or shared baseline.

Playback:

- multiple identifiable edge approaches;
- routes converge into stable zones;
- short local circulation, not stationary micro-drift;
- route-owned exit;
- at most twelve simultaneous prints even when all twenty-four identities participate.

## Topology

`ordered perimeter-route field + structural exclusion zone + bounded source-order cohorts`

This topology must survive without colour, captions, shadows, filters, or decorative background.

## Invariants

1. Every source owns one immutable route ID.
2. Route start, field end, cohort, and source order are deterministic.
3. No source jumps between zones.
4. Negative-space exclusion is compiled and testable.
5. Every accepted identity appears during the complete phrase.
6. At most twelve are visible/renderable.
7. Failed slots retain route and index.
8. Source opacity/filter/blend remain 1/none/normal.
9. Reverse is exact retrace.
10. Seam starts and ends outside the canvas with equal state.

## Anti-goals

- random scatter or masonry packing;
- periodic slot remap;
- wall-clock intervals;
- source dimming to manufacture focus;
- screensaver drift;
- clone decks for infinite travel;
- Quiet Drift with higher amplitude;
- ticker, carousel, grid, or stack logic;
- density control that duplicates source-count meaning.

## Material roles

- source image: protected;
- print edge: Scene apparatus;
- perimeter route: motion geometry;
- field zone: stable authored destination;
- negative-space exclusion: composition structure;
- focus plane: temporary physical separation;
- Look/background: external.

## Deletion test

Deleting this directory removes the full route-field study without affecting Quiet Drift or Product. No shared scatter engine exists. A common primitive is not earned until two accepted S1 implementations pass a deletion test without identity collapse.

# Scene DNA — Quiet Drift

## Memorable outcome

A recognisable, low-relief cluster of proofs occupies one shared table. Every print has a place. The arrangement breathes without travelling. Attention moves through physical lift and return, not through source treatment.

## Irreducible visual signature

Silhouette-only still:

- overlapping but non-collapsed print rectangles;
- unequal ratios and slight authored rotations;
- one compact centre of gravity;
- stable neighbourhoods rather than a lane, orbit, grid, pile, or perimeter field.

Silhouette-only playback:

- separate tiny closed paths;
- no group translation;
- no arrivals from edges;
- one temporary focus plane;
- whole composition damping toward stillness before an exact seam.

Remove any of those and the Scene becomes either a generic static collage or Lively Prints with lower amplitude.

## Topology

`stable neighbourhood cluster + independent loop-closed local fields`

Topology is the primary distinction from Lively Prints. Quiet Drift never owns perimeter routes, cohort pages, edge arrivals, or a negative-space exclusion zone. Motion occurs around remembered places, not between outside and inside.

## Spatial roles

- **Source plane:** imported artwork inside its Project-owned frame intent.
- **Paper plane:** restrained physical edge around the source; no lighting.
- **Base table plane:** stable authored z-order and overlap graph.
- **Focus plane:** temporary non-destructive lift above the base plane.
- **Look plane:** outside the Scene; may be solid, transparent, or later authored.

## Temporal roles

- **Breath engagement:** field amplitude rises while composition remains present.
- **Micro-current:** decorrelated local loops.
- **Attention:** one source lifts and returns.
- **Finale:** motion withdraws; attention gathers through stillness.
- **Return:** focus plane closes back into the base stack before the seam.

## Invariants

1. Ordered IDs never change.
2. Base positions, rotations, and z values never change after compilation.
3. Every trajectory is a closed function of normalized story time.
4. Non-focused z-order is immutable.
5. Focus enters a separate plane; it never mutates source order.
6. Source opacity is 1, filter is `none`, blend is `normal`.
7. Failed items retain position and index.
8. Transparent pixels remain colourless.
9. One and two sources are designed compositions, not degenerate many-source logic.
10. Evaluated work is bounded at twelve source states.

## Anti-goals

- random packing;
- periodic reshuffle or “deal to back”;
- pointer parallax;
- wall-clock animation inside the evaluator;
- elastic spring collisions;
- generic hover theatre;
- source dimming around the active card;
- glow, zoom, or vignette finale;
- table texture masquerading as Scene identity;
- shared scatter engine with Lively Prints.

## Default character

- intimate, not spectacular;
- physically plausible enough to feel authored, not simulated;
- slight asymmetry without chaos;
- readable source art before motion system;
- final stillness as punctuation.

## Deletion test

Delete the entire `drift-deck` directory. No other atelier prototype, Product module, registry entry, package file, or shared contract breaks. Conversely, deleting Lively Prints must not remove Quiet Drift’s evaluator, controls, fixture rules, or evidence. The current study therefore earns no shared scatter primitive.

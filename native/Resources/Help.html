Galileo Gallery

A silent motion studio for your slides, images and video.

Begin

Use Add or drop images, videos or PDF pages. Choose Scene to audition a family and variant. Cancel leaves the document unchanged.

Direct

Select media and enable Bring to centre. Set Hold, Size and Transition. Preview spotlight auditions it; the previous/next spotlight controls jump between holds. Use as closing adds a finite final hold. Video can keep looping while a slide stays centred; editor Pause freezes the whole preview.

Background

In the Scene inspector, open Canvas and choose Background → Drift. Browse by family or name and choose Use background. Palette, colour and texture affect the background only. Turn off Animate background to freeze its motion and grain without changing source-video playback. Solid, gradient and transparent canvases remain available.

Frame

Edit framing opens crop handles, aspect lock and fill positioning. Fit preserves the complete crop; Fill fills the chosen frame ratio. Mixed indicates different values across selected media. Use Fit or 100% canvas zoom and pan with the trackpad.

Save and recover

Save creates a native document with its media. Undo and autosave use the Mac document system. If media is missing, open a recovery copy and Locate original or Replace it. Unresolved included media cannot export. Version 2.4 saves schema 8. Older native projects open as protected upgrade copies; save to a new destination. Earlier releases cannot read schema 8, and the original remains intact.

Export

Choose a whole sequence, one selected spotlight, a time range or a still. H.264 and ProRes 422 are opaque; ProRes 4444 and PNG support transparency. The queue runs one job at a time. Movies are silent by design. Original imported videos are not stripped or rewritten.

Keys

Space plays or pauses when the canvas has focus. ← and → step frames. Use the standard File and Edit menus for saving, opening and undo.

Full instructions and known limits are in the repository's current native guide and release notes.

WebP and WebM

Still and animated WebP import locally. SDR VP8/VP9 WebM is prepared with bundled tools, including transparent VP9. The original stays unchanged. AV1 and HDR WebM are not supported. Native working copies can be larger than the compressed source. Display chooses moving Video or Animation, or a remembered Still: First, Middle, Last or Custom. Still starts with Last. Select one clip and use Preview clip for a silent filmstrip, trims and looping. For a custom still, pause on the loaded picture, choose Use this frame, then Apply. Cancel leaves the document unchanged.
